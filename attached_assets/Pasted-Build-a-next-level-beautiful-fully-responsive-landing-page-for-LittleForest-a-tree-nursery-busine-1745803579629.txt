Build a next-level, beautiful, fully responsive landing page for LittleForest, a tree nursery business.

Keep the original natural color scheme (greens, browns, natural earth tones) but make it more modern and airy with lots of white space.

Design the following sections:

Sticky Navigation Bar

Logo on the left (LittleForest logo)

Menu items: Home | Trees | Blog | About | Contact

Highlight the current page.

Hero Section (Full Screen Height)

Background: Large, soft-focus photo of a vibrant forest or young trees.

Headline: "Plant Today, Grow Tomorrow"

Subheadline: "Nurture your dreams with LittleForest's premium trees."

Primary button: "Browse Trees" (green button)

Secondary button: "Make an Inquiry" (outlined button)

Smooth scroll effect down to the next section.

Featured Trees Section

Title: "Our Favorites"

3–6 tree cards with images, names, and short, poetic descriptions.

Cards should have slight hover animations (scale up a little).

Why Choose LittleForest? (Trust Section)

Three trust badges/icons:

"Sustainably Grown"

"Locally Sourced"

"1000+ Trees Planted"

Short sentences under each badge.

About Us Section

Split layout:

Left: Image of tree nursery or people planting.

Right: Paragraph about LittleForest's mission, sustainability, and love for nature.

Mini Blog Section

Title: "From Our Forest Journal"

Display 3 latest blog posts with image thumbnails, titles, short excerpts, and 'Read More' links.

Order & Inquiry Form Section

Title: "Let's Grow Together"

Short description inviting customers to reach out.

Form fields: Name, Email, Phone (optional), Tree Type (dropdown), Message (textarea).

Validation for email and required fields.

Submit button (big, green).

Footer

Quick links: Home, Trees, Blog, Contact.

Social media icons (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn).

Style Guidelines:

Use TailwindCSS for rapid, clean styling.

Rounded cards and buttons, soft box shadows, clean sans-serif font (e.g., 'Poppins').

Fade-in animations for sections as the user scrolls down.

Fully mobile responsive (stacked sections on smaller screens).

Technical Additions:

Add a meta title and meta description for SEO ("LittleForest - Premium Trees for Every Dream").

Add alt tags to every image.

Optimize all images for faster loading.
